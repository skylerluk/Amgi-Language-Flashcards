Headword levels and reference pinyin were checked against Complete HSK Vocabulary:
https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
MIT license reproduced in HSK-DATA-LICENSE.txt.
The HSK labels use the six-level HSK 2.0 system (old-3, old-4, old-5).
Entries were selected using the dataset's frequency ordering and edited for everyday usefulness.
Meanings, examples, usage notes, and production prompts were drafted for Amgi and reviewed for consistency.
The phrase pack contains original curated expressions and grammar patterns, with approximate intermediate difficulty.
These are selected practice decks, not complete official exam wordlists or a promise of exam readiness.
